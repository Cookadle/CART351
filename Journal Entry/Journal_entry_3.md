# Journal Entry Week 3

I have had very little interest in coding this semester so far. I could say it might be due to the conditions of life and the state of the world, but I get to blame an even greater villain: capitalism, or work, as many would say.

This week, I made it my goal to force myself not to have fun. However, I am also behind on my project, so I am now in the process of brainstorming.

I think humans leave a lot unsaid. In the midst of getting diagnosed with autism, I noticed that being upfront and direct might actually scare people. So, my concept relies on people and asks a simple question: Are you more honest when you are hidden behind a screen and a pseudonym?

Inspired by Queering the Map, I thought about creating something similar, but instead of mapping queer experiences, it would collect confessions. However, rather than making it a classic confession forum, I could use the data entered by users to generate images or perhaps create a wall of moving text.

I think we can all agree that we are entering an era of late-stage capitalism. Being an influencer is now a job in which people perform and sell their morals for their next month's rent. I don't necessarily shame them for it, but given where we seem to be heading, it feels rational to create a project in which the data provided by users sells them an experience.

I could classify people based on their answers or transform their data into a garden of flowers.

Collective human input has to be the central theme, partly because of one of the readings, We Feel Fine. The idea of collecting people's statements online and transforming them into interactive art, with the experience shaped by the individuals themselves, is fascinating to me.

I believe in the idea of a shared consciousness. Being able to re-emulate this through technology, a tool that has often been said to sever community and human experience, is a challenge I am excited to take on.

Anyhow, I'll leave my train of thought here. :)
