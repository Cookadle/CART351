#The Oracle Confession App
You submit something you never said.
I shall read it to you as one read tea leaves.
You shall receive a fortune calibrated to you.(yes it is true)

A new perpective on occults arts such as tarot,fortune reading and the likes.Who are you to your computer once the data collects?Is it trully you or what you wanted to hear?How accurate is it?

A modern take on using computer/ai to do ocult art such as reading tarots and reading fortunes and palms,brewing teas and the like.How a pratice that is usually made and praticed with physical presence and intent get replaced.

Inspired by the rumour where Skyla shared a story on how chatgpt was reading tea leaves and gave their friend a reading.(Never comfirmed with her but i thought it was cyberpunk dystopian so I reframed it)

Also inspired by my previous project to do list where the data of the user and the experience is didacted by the user info rather than the web.
