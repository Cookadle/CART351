MySpace by Nerly 
I love the graphic the colour are really cute vibrant and reminiscent of 2000 early web 2.0
I appreciate how they were able to convey the fun feeling of feelings out your information on-line such as MySpace. I used to be excited to fill out those type of form. But I do feel like its lacking an original concept,I feel like I’m seeing a prototype of what my space is to be as if the creator was showing the early prototype rather than its own application. I think data being stolen across MySpace profile and getting notification like ”your favourite music was stolen by this user” or “your age has been replaced by this user. You could make the user write a secret note and then someone would post “did you know that user name biggest secret is…..” .Making the social application less about being a reminder of MySpace but rather the user experience.

Journal entry by Nat
I think our concept are similar in sense of how the data of the users is used as the content. I think its interesting because we had similar ideas such as registering the time it took to type and identifying the words such as verb and pronouns,Logging hesitation or if something was erased then written are both concept I wanted to implement in my code but didn’t know how it could impact the experience. Its a very neat and extremely categorized way of sorting and organizing information. The simple design is also perfectly fitted for this. I feel like this web site could have people enter entries and follow their stories. I could see someone writing an horror story using journal entry as pages and we get to analyse how they felt at that moment through all those attributes added such as such emotions and the hesitation in their entries.

Are we sharing too much by Avi and Sky
mini-project-1
I love the use of a 3d model. I think many of us have the 3d class so its fun to see how it seep into other class project. I love how a object that is meant to serve us and satisfy our need has been changed into something that consume and control which is a great analogy of how our data is being used. Very interesting way of using the API to get really personal details such as the WiFi and the scanning of the files. It feels really intrusive and violating with fall in line with the goal of exposing how our data is being taken and analysed to pinpoint things such as location etc. I like how AI/LLM was used in this project as a way to enhance the project experience. I am very excited to see this project being pushed if they continue with this.
